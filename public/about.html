About Tengesa Marketplace

Connecting Buyers & Sellers Seamlessly — Buy, Sell & Grow with Confidence.

Our story

A more connected way to trade.

Tengesa Marketplace is a digital platform concept built to make it easier for people and organisations to discover products, connect with sellers and explore commercial opportunities.

Rooted in Harare, Zimbabwe, Tengesa is designed with a wider outlook: accessible discovery, useful tools and a clear experience for buyers and vendors.

Trust & safety

Confidence is built through good practice.

  • Clear listing information and seller communication
  • Encouragement to verify products and sellers before purchase
  • Channels for support and reporting concerns
  • Ongoing improvements to marketplace safeguards

Tengesa does not guarantee individual sellers or transactions through this starter website. Buyers should independently verify goods, sellers and payment arrangements.
